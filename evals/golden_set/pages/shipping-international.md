# International Shipping

## Standard International Shipping

Standard international shipping costs $19.99 and takes 10 to 18 business days depending on destination customs processing. We currently ship to over 40 countries. Orders may be subject to import duties and taxes charged by the destination country, which the customer is responsible for paying.

## Expedited International Shipping

Expedited international shipping costs $39.99 and arrives in 3 to 5 business days to most destinations. A small number of remote destinations may take up to 7 business days even on the expedited option.

## Customs and Restricted Items

Some items cannot be shipped internationally due to battery or customs restrictions in certain countries. The product page for an item will say if international shipping is unavailable for that item.
