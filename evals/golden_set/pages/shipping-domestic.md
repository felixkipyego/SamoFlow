# Shipping (Domestic)

## Standard Shipping

Standard domestic shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and costs $4.99 on orders under $50. Orders of $50 or more ship free. We pack every order in recycled materials and ship from our main warehouse, usually within one business day of the order being placed.

## Expedited Shipping

Expedited shipping costs $14.99 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. It is available on all domestic orders, including orders that already qualify for free standard shipping. Expedited orders placed before 2pm local time usually ship the same day.

## Order Tracking

Every shipped order gets a tracking number by email within 24 hours of shipment. Tracking links go to our carrier partner's own tracking page.
