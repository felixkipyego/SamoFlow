# Account Help

## Resetting Your Password

Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter the email address on your account. We'll send a reset link that's valid for 24 hours. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder before contacting support.

## Updating Your Shipping Address

You can add or edit a shipping address any time from the Addresses tab in your account. Changes only apply to future orders; an order already placed keeps the address it was placed with.

## Closing Your Account

Contact support to close your account. We'll keep order history for a short period as required for tax and accounting purposes, then delete the rest of your personal data.
