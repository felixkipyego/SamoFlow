# Gift Cards

## Buying a Gift Card

Digital gift cards are available in $25, $50, $100, and $200 denominations and are delivered by email within minutes of purchase. Gift cards never expire and carry no maintenance fees.

## Redeeming a Gift Card

Enter the gift card code at checkout in the "Gift card or promo code" field. A gift card balance can be combined with one other gift card on the same order, but not with a percentage-off promo code.

## Gift Card Balance

Check your remaining gift card balance any time on the Gift Cards page in your account, or by entering the code at checkout without completing the order.
