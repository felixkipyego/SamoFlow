# Store Hours and Locations

## Online Store

We are an online-only store; there is no physical retail location to visit. The online store is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and orders can be placed any time.

## Warehouse Pickup

A small number of large items, like major appliances, offer free local pickup from our warehouse on weekdays between 9am and 4pm. Pickup availability shows on the product page at checkout if it applies to that item.

## Holiday Shipping Cutoffs

During major holidays we post a shipping cutoff date on the homepage banner; orders placed after that date may not arrive before the holiday even with expedited shipping selected.
