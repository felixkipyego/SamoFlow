# Product Care

## Cleaning Electronics

Unplug a device before cleaning it, and use a dry or barely-damp microfiber cloth only; never spray liquid directly onto a device. Avoid alcohol-based cleaners on screens with an anti-glare coating, since they can strip the coating over time.

## Storing Seasonal Items

Store seasonal appliances in their original box if you still have it, in a dry area away from direct sunlight. Remove batteries from any battery-powered item before long-term storage to avoid corrosion damage.

## Firmware Updates

Smart home devices occasionally receive firmware updates over Wi-Fi; keep the companion app installed and notifications enabled so you don't miss an important security update.
