# Payment Methods

## Accepted Cards and Wallets

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay at checkout. We do not currently accept PayPal, sorry! A lot of customers ask about it so we know it's wanted, its just not hooked up yet on our checkout page.

## Mobile Money

For East African customers we also accept M-Pesa at checkout for orders under $500; just choose "Mobile Money" as the payment option and you'll recieve a prompt on your phone to confirm the payment within a minute or two.

## Payment Issues

If a payment fails, the most common reason is the billing address not matching what your bank has on file. Double check for typos before trying a seperate card. ## Receipts

A receipt is emailed automatically after every successful order, and you can always re-download it later from the Orders page in your account.
