# Warranty Policy

## Overview

Every product we sell comes with a manufacturer warranty, and most product categories also carry an additional store warranty on top of it, which we describe in detail below. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use, starting from the date of delivery rather than the date of purchase, since delivery is when the customer actually begins using the product. A warranty claim does not require the original packaging, although keeping it does make a replacement shipment easier for us to arrange, since we can reuse the same box and padding when we send a new unit back to you. To start a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a short description of the problem, and for most electronics we will also ask for a photo or a short video showing the fault, since this lets our technical team triage the issue correctly on the first message instead of going back and forth over several emails. We aim to respond to every warranty claim within two business days, and most claims are resolved, meaning a replacement is shipped or a repair is scheduled, within ten business days of the first message. Warranty coverage is tied to the original purchasing account and is not transferable if you resell or give away the product to someone else, with the single exception of major appliances, where the warranty follows the physical unit itself rather than the account that purchased it, since appliances are commonly sold second-hand as part of a house move. Accidental damage, including drops, liquid spills, and power surges, is not covered by the standard warranty described here, although some product categories offer a separate paid accidental damage plan at checkout, which is described on that product's own page rather than in this general policy. Wear items, such as batteries, cables, and foam ear pads, are only covered if they fail within the first thirty days; after that they are considered normal wear and are not eligible for a free warranty replacement. If a replacement unit is sent under warranty, the replacement itself carries the remainder of the original warranty period, not a brand new full warranty term, so warranty coverage does not reset just because a unit was swapped. International customers are covered by the same warranty terms described here, but may need to arrange their own return shipping for a warranty claim, depending on the destination country, since our prepaid return labels are a domestic-only service today. We keep a record of every warranty claim tied to the order, so a second claim on the same unit does not need you to re-explain the original issue from scratch, as our support team can see the full history when you contact us again.

## Warranty Duration by Category

| Category | Store Warranty | Manufacturer Warranty |
|---|---|---|
| Headphones and speakers | 1 year | 1 year |
| Smart home devices | 2 years | 1 year |
| Major appliances | 3 years | 2 years |
| Small kitchen gadgets | 1 year | 90 days |
| Accesories (cables, cases) | 90 days | 90 days |

## Claims Outside Your Country

A customer who moved countries after buying a product shuold still contact support with the original order number; the warranty itself does not expire just because the customer relocated, but repair turnaround times can be longer if we need to ship a replacement internationally.
