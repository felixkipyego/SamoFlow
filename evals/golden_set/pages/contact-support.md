# Contact and Support

## Live Chat

Live chat is staffed weekdays from 8am to 6pm East Africa Time, and Saturdays from 9am to 1pm. Outside those hours the widget still answers from our help content, and you can leave a message for a human to see when chat reopens.

## Email Support

Email support@acme-gadgets.example and we usually reply within one business day.
